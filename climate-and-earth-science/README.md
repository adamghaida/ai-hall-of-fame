# 🌍 Climate & Earth Science

AI weather models that beat the supercomputer-powered systems the world has relied on for decades.

---

### GraphCast — 10-day global forecasts in under a minute

- **What happened:** DeepMind's **GraphCast**, a graph-neural-network model trained on decades of reanalysis data, predicts hundreds of weather variables 10 days ahead at 0.25° resolution — and outperformed the leading operational deterministic system (ECMWF's HRES) on **90% of 1,380 verification targets**. It produces a forecast in **under a minute** on a single machine, versus hours on a supercomputer.
- **When:** November 2023 (*Science*).
- **Why it's a big deal:** Better, dramatically cheaper forecasts — including improved tracking of cyclones, atmospheric rivers, and extreme heat — with direct implications for disaster preparedness.
- **Sources:** [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/graphcast-ai-model-for-faster-and-more-accurate-global-weather-forecasting/) · [Science paper](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.adi2336)

### GenCast — state-of-the-art ensemble forecasting

- **What happened:** **GenCast**, a diffusion-based ensemble model, generates probabilistic forecasts up to 15 days ahead and beat ECMWF's top ensemble system (ENS) on **97.2% of verification targets** — and on **99.8%** at lead times beyond 36 hours.
- **When:** December 2024 (*Nature*).
- **Why it's a big deal:** Ensemble forecasts quantify *uncertainty and risk* — crucial for extreme-weather warnings — and GenCast set a new bar while running far faster and cheaper than physics-based ensembles.
- **Sources:** [DeepMind blog](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/gencast-predicts-weather-and-the-risks-of-extreme-conditions-with-sota-accuracy/) · [Nature paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08252-9)
