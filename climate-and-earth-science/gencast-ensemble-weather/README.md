# GenCast — state-of-the-art ensemble weather forecasting

**Field:** 🌍 Climate & Earth Science  ·  **When:** December 2024 · Nature

> A diffusion model that beat the top ensemble forecast on 97% of targets.

## What happened
**GenCast**, a diffusion-based ensemble model, generates probabilistic forecasts up to 15 days ahead and beat ECMWF's top ensemble system (ENS) on **97.2% of verification targets** — and **99.8%** at lead times beyond 36 hours.

## Why it matters
Ensembles quantify *uncertainty and risk*, which is what matters most for extreme-weather warnings. GenCast set a new bar while running far faster and cheaper than physics-based ensembles.
