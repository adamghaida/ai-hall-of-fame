# 📚 Context: GenCast — state-of-the-art ensemble weather forecasting

The move from a single deterministic forecast (GraphCast) to a calibrated *distribution* of forecasts (GenCast) is the key advance — weather is chaotic, so a good spread of scenarios beats one crisp-but-overconfident prediction, especially for rare, high-impact events.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Ensembles quantify *uncertainty and risk*, which is what matters most for extreme-weather warnings. GenCast set a new bar while running far faster and cheaper than physics-based ensembles.
