# DGMR — 'nowcasting' the next 90 minutes of rain

**Field:** 🌍 Climate & Earth Science  ·  **When:** September 2021 · Nature

> Meteorologists preferred the AI's rain forecasts 89% of the time.

## What happened
DeepMind and the UK Met Office built **DGMR**, a deep generative model of radar that predicts high-resolution rainfall for the next **90 minutes** from the past 20 minutes of radar. In a study with **58 expert meteorologists**, DGMR ranked first for accuracy and usefulness in **89%** of cases versus existing methods.

## Why it matters
Short-term, local rainfall prediction ('nowcasting') is vital for flood warnings, transport, and energy — and is exactly where traditional physics models struggle.
