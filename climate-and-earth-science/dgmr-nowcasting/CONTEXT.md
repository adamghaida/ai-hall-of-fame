# 📚 Context: DGMR — 'nowcasting' the next 90 minutes of rain

The generative approach matters: earlier ML nowcasters produced blurry, over-smoothed rain that averaged away exactly the sharp convective cells forecasters care about. By generating sharp, spatially-consistent scenarios, DGMR gave outputs meteorologists actually trusted — a case where expert human evaluation, not just error metrics, defined success.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Short-term, local rainfall prediction ('nowcasting') is vital for flood warnings, transport, and energy — and is exactly where traditional physics models struggle.
