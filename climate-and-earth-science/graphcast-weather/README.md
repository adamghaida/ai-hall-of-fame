# GraphCast — 10-day global forecasts in under a minute

**Field:** 🌍 Climate & Earth Science  ·  **When:** November 2023 · Science

> An AI weather model that beat the world's best operational system on 90% of targets.

## What happened
DeepMind's **GraphCast**, a graph-neural-network model trained on decades of reanalysis data, predicts hundreds of weather variables 10 days ahead at 0.25° resolution and outperformed ECMWF's leading deterministic system (HRES) on **90% of 1,380 verification targets** — producing a forecast in **under a minute** on a single machine.

## Why it matters
Better, dramatically cheaper forecasts — including improved cyclone tracking and extreme-heat prediction — with direct implications for disaster preparedness.
