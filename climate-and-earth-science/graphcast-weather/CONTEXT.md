# 📚 Context: GraphCast — 10-day global forecasts in under a minute

GraphCast forecasts *from* an analysis (initial state) still produced by traditional systems, so it complements rather than fully replaces physics-based numerical weather prediction. Its success helped trigger a wave of ML weather models (Pangu-Weather, FuXi, Aurora, and ECMWF's own AIFS) now moving into operations.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Better, dramatically cheaper forecasts — including improved cyclone tracking and extreme-heat prediction — with direct implications for disaster preparedness.
