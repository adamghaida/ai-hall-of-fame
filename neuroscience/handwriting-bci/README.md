# Handwriting BCI — thinking letters onto a screen

**Field:** 🧠 Neuroscience  ·  **When:** May 2021 · Nature

> Imagined handwriting decoded into text at 90 characters per minute.

## What happened
A Stanford/BrainGate team decoded **attempted handwriting** from motor-cortex activity of a man with a paralyzed hand, using a recurrent neural network. He typed at **90 characters per minute** with 94% raw accuracy (>99% with autocorrect) — more than double the previous BCI typing record.

## Why it matters
By decoding the fast, distinctive neural patterns of *imagined writing* rather than slow point-and-click, it dramatically raised the communication speed possible for people with paralysis.
