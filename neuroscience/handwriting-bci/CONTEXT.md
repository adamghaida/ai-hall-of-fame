# 📚 Context: Handwriting BCI — thinking letters onto a screen

The clever insight was choosing a *temporally rich* motor task: distinct pen strokes create more separable neural signatures than trying to 'move a cursor', so the decoder can tell letters apart. It set the stage for the 2023 speech neuroprostheses (see [Medicine](../../medicine/)) from overlapping teams.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
By decoding the fast, distinctive neural patterns of *imagined writing* rather than slow point-and-click, it dramatically raised the communication speed possible for people with paralysis.
