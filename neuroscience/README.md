# 🧠 Neuroscience

Decoding intention directly from the brain — and turning it back into speech.

---

### High-performance speech neuroprostheses

- **What happened:** In back-to-back 2023 *Nature* papers, teams at **Stanford** and **UCSF/UC Berkeley** used deep-learning decoders to translate neural activity from people with severe paralysis into text and audible speech in near real time. The Stanford system reached **62 words per minute** (3.4× the prior record, approaching natural conversation) for a participant with ALS; the UCSF system decoded speech *and* controlled an expressive talking **avatar** for a participant paralyzed by a brainstem stroke. A 2025 follow-up produced streaming **brain-to-voice** synthesis in the person's own restored voice.
- **When:** August 2023 (with 2025 extensions).
- **Why it's a big deal:** These systems reconnect people who cannot speak to the world at conversational speed — a landmark in both neuroscience and assistive technology, powered by AI models that learn each person's unique neural code.
- **Sources:** [Stanford / Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06377-x) · [UCSF / Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06443-4) · [Berkeley Engineering (brain-to-voice)](https://engineering.berkeley.edu/news/2025/03/brain-to-voice-neuroprosthesis-restores-naturalistic-speech/)
