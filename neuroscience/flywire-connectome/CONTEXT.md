# 📚 Context: FlyWire — the first complete wiring map of an adult brain

AI did the impossible-by-hand part — tracing every wire through a 3D EM volume — but human proofreaders were essential to fix errors and assign cell types, another 'centaur' success. Connectomics scales steeply: a mouse brain is ~1,000× larger, so the fly is both a triumph and a preview of the compute-and-labor challenge ahead.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It's the most complex whole-brain wiring diagram ever produced — a foundational reference for understanding how neural circuits generate behavior, and a stepping stone toward larger brains.
