# FlyWire — the first complete wiring map of an adult brain

**Field:** 🧠 Neuroscience  ·  **When:** October 2024 · Nature

> AI segmentation mapped 140,000 neurons and 50 million connections — a whole brain.

## What happened
The **FlyWire** consortium reconstructed the first complete connectome of an adult **fruit fly** brain: ~**139,000 neurons** and **>50 million synapses**. AI automatically segmented neurons from electron-microscope images, and a 200+ person community proofread and annotated **8,453 cell types** (over half of them new).

## Why it matters
It's the most complex whole-brain wiring diagram ever produced — a foundational reference for understanding how neural circuits generate behavior, and a stepping stone toward larger brains.
