# OpenAI Five — beating the Dota 2 world champions

**Field:** ♟️ Games & Strategy  ·  **When:** April 2019

> The first AI to beat reigning world champions at an esport, live on stream.

## What happened
**OpenAI Five** played 5v5 Dota 2, a long-horizon team game of hidden information, using large-scale self-play (PPO) trained with roughly 770 petaflop/s-days of compute over months. In April 2019 it beat reigning world champions **Team OG** 2-0, then won 99.4% of games against the public in an online showcase.

## Why it matters
Dota 2 combines an enormous action space, very long time horizons (tens of thousands of decisions per game), hidden information, and five-agent coordination. Beating world champions showed self-play reinforcement learning scaling to messy, real-time team games.
