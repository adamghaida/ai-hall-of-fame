# 📚 Context: OpenAI Five — beating the Dota 2 world champions

Two ideas carried it: massive-scale distributed PPO self-play, and 'surgery' to keep training through changes to the model and the game itself over ten months. Caveats: the hero pool and some items/mechanics were restricted, and the compute bill was enormous. It is the team-game companion to the AlphaStar entry in this field.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Dota 2 combines an enormous action space, very long time horizons (tens of thousands of decisions per game), hidden information, and five-agent coordination. Beating world champions showed self-play reinforcement learning scaling to messy, real-time team games.
