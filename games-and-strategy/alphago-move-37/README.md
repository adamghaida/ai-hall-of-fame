# AlphaGo & 'Move 37'

**Field:** ♟️ Games & Strategy  ·  **When:** March 2016

> The moment AI showed genuine creativity, not just calculation.

## What happened
DeepMind's **AlphaGo** beat 18-time world champion **Lee Sedol** 4–1 at Go. In game 2 it played **Move 37**, a shoulder-hit so unconventional that commentators assumed it was a mistake — a move with roughly a **1-in-10,000** chance of being chosen by a human — yet it proved brilliant.

## Why it matters
Go's astronomical complexity and reliance on intuition made it a grand challenge for AI. Move 37 is remembered as the moment a machine produced original strategy no human had taught it, even reshaping human Go theory.
