# 📚 Context: AlphaGo & 'Move 37'

AlphaGo combined supervised learning from human games with reinforcement learning and search. Its successor AlphaGo Zero dropped human data entirely, and AlphaZero generalized to chess and shogi — the lineage that later powered AlphaTensor, AlphaDev, and plasma control. Move 37 became shorthand for AI surprising its own creators.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Go's astronomical complexity and reliance on intuition made it a grand challenge for AI. Move 37 is remembered as the moment a machine produced original strategy no human had taught it, even reshaping human Go theory.
