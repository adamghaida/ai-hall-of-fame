# AlphaZero — mastering Go, chess, and shogi from scratch

**Field:** ♟️ Games & Strategy  ·  **When:** 2017–2018 · Science

> Given only the rules, it surpassed the best programs in hours of self-play.

## What happened
**AlphaZero** learned Go, chess, and shogi entirely through **self-play**, starting from random moves and **zero human game data**. Within hours it surpassed the strongest existing programs, playing a strikingly dynamic, sacrificial style that reshaped human opening theory.

## Why it matters
A single, general self-play algorithm reached superhuman play across three very different games — the conceptual engine behind much of this repository.
