# 📚 Context: AlphaZero — mastering Go, chess, and shogi from scratch

AlphaZero's philosophical punch was doing away with human knowledge and hand-crafted evaluation functions entirely — just rules, search, and self-play. Its chess games were studied by top grandmasters for their unusual, positional-sacrifice style, and its generality (same algorithm, three games) is what made it a template for scientific problems framed as games.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
A single, general self-play algorithm reached superhuman play across three very different games — the conceptual engine behind much of this repository.
