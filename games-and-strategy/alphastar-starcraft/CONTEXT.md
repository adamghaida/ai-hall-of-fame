# 📚 Context: AlphaStar — Grandmaster at StarCraft II

The 'league' training was the key idea: rather than one agent, AlphaStar maintained a population including 'exploiter' agents that hunted the main agent's weaknesses, preventing the strategic rock-paper-scissors collapse that plagues self-play in imperfect-information games. It was also constrained to human-like camera and action-rate limits to keep the comparison fair.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
StarCraft combines real-time control, imperfect information, long horizons, and an enormous action space — a very different, messier challenge than turn-based board games.
