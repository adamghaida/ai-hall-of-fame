# DeepNash — expert Stratego without any search

**Field:** ♟️ Games & Strategy  ·  **When:** December 2022 · Science

> Mastering a game with a game tree 10^175 times larger than Go's.

## What happened
DeepMind's **DeepNash** learned to play **Stratego** — which mixes chess-like long-term strategy with poker-like hidden information — at human-expert level using **model-free** reinforcement learning and **no search**. It reached a top-three all-time ranking on the Gravon online platform.

## Why it matters
Stratego's game tree (~10^535 nodes) and imperfect information defeat the tree-search methods behind AlphaZero; DeepNash showed a different route — directly learning a game-theoretic (Nash) strategy.
