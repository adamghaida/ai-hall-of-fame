# 📚 Context: DeepNash — expert Stratego without any search

DeepNash is the counterpoint to AlphaZero: when the state space is too large and information too hidden for lookahead search, you can still converge to strong, unexploitable play by following dynamics that provably head toward equilibrium. Its deceptive bluffs and information-hiding emerged naturally from that objective.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Stratego's game tree (~10^535 nodes) and imperfect information defeat the tree-search methods behind AlphaZero; DeepNash showed a different route — directly learning a game-theoretic (Nash) strategy.
