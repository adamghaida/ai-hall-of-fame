# Gran Turismo Sophy — outracing the world's best sim drivers

**Field:** ♟️ Games & Strategy  ·  **When:** February 2022 · Nature

> A racing AI that beat champion e-sport drivers wheel-to-wheel, cleanly.

## What happened
Sony AI's **Gran Turismo Sophy** became the first superhuman agent to **outrace the world's best drivers** in the highly realistic *Gran Turismo* racing simulator, mastering not just optimal racing lines but close, rule-abiding wheel-to-wheel racing tactics.

## Why it matters
Racing at the limit demands split-second continuous control *and* nuanced etiquette (overtaking without causing collisions) — a hard blend of precision control and multi-agent judgment.
