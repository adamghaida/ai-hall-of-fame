# 📚 Context: Gran Turismo Sophy — outracing the world's best sim drivers

The research contribution wasn't just speed — plenty of bots can hug a racing line. It was encoding *racing etiquette* so the agent could pass and defend against humans without dirty driving, which required reward shaping informed by the sport's unwritten rules. A neat example of aligning an agent with human norms, not just a score.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Racing at the limit demands split-second continuous control *and* nuanced etiquette (overtaking without causing collisions) — a hard blend of precision control and multi-agent judgment.
