# Pluribus — superhuman six-player poker

**Field:** ♟️ Games & Strategy  ·  **When:** July 2019 · Science

> The first AI to beat pros at multiplayer poker — the long-standing open case.

## What happened
**Pluribus** (CMU + Facebook AI) beat elite professionals at **six-player no-limit Texas Hold'em**, the most popular form of poker. Prior breakthroughs handled only two players; multiplayer imperfect-information games were the recognized open challenge.

## Why it matters
It cracked a decades-old milestone in game theory and AI — and did so cheaply, training in days on modest hardware rather than a supercomputer.
