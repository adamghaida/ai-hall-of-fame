# 📚 Context: CICERO — human-level play in Diplomacy, negotiation included

A notable design goal was *honesty/consistency*: CICERO was trained so its messages aligned with its actual planned moves, rather than learning to freely deceive — both for performance (trust helps in Diplomacy) and for responsible-AI reasons. It remains a striking demonstration of language + strategy, though it can still be exploited by adversarial human play.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Diplomacy demands cooperation, persuasion, and strategic reasoning together — the first game-playing AI to combine grounded natural-language dialogue with long-horizon planning against humans.
