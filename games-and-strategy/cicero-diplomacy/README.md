# CICERO — human-level play in Diplomacy, negotiation included

**Field:** ♟️ Games & Strategy  ·  **When:** November 2022 · Science

> An AI that had to *talk* its way to victory, blending language and strategy.

## What happened
Meta's **CICERO** reached human-level performance in **Diplomacy**, a seven-player game of alliances won through **natural-language negotiation**. It fused a language model with planning and RL, inferring other players' intentions and generating dialogue to pursue its plans — scoring more than double the human average across 40 online games.

## Why it matters
Diplomacy demands cooperation, persuasion, and strategic reasoning together — the first game-playing AI to combine grounded natural-language dialogue with long-horizon planning against humans.
