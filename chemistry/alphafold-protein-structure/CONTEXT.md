# 📚 Context: AlphaFold — solving protein folding (Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2024)

AlphaFold2's key ideas were the Evoformer (reasoning jointly over an MSA and residue-pair representations) and end-to-end training to predict 3D coordinates with a structure module. It essentially ended the decades-long CASP structure-prediction competition as an open contest. Caveats remain: it predicts static structures, handles dynamics/complexes less well, and AF3 extends toward interactions with ligands, DNA and RNA.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Protein shape determines function, so this reshaped biology, drug discovery, and enzyme engineering overnight. It was the first Nobel recognizing a breakthrough enabled by AI, and the AlphaFold database has served millions of researchers in 190+ countries.
