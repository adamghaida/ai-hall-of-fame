# AlphaFold — solving protein folding (Nobel Prize in Chemistry 2024)

**Field:** 🧪 Chemistry  ·  **When:** 2020–2021 · Nobel 2024

> A 50-year grand challenge solved by AI — and recognized with a Nobel Prize.

## What happened
AlphaFold2 predicts a protein's 3D structure from its amino-acid sequence with near-experimental accuracy (~1Å backbone). DeepMind then released predicted structures for **~200 million proteins** — nearly every catalogued protein — free to all researchers. Demis Hassabis and John Jumper shared the **2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry** (with David Baker for protein design).

## Why it matters
Protein shape determines function, so this reshaped biology, drug discovery, and enzyme engineering overnight. It was the first Nobel recognizing a breakthrough enabled by AI, and the AlphaFold database has served millions of researchers in 190+ countries.
