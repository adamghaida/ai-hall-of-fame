# 📚 Context: Halicin — a new antibiotic found by deep learning

The important subtlety: the model was trained only on growth inhibition, so it found molecules that *work*, not molecules that resemble known antibiotics — which is exactly why halicin's scaffold was novel. It still required wet-lab validation and is not an approved drug; the achievement is the discovery pipeline, not a finished medicine.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Antibiotic discovery had nearly dried up. AI screening revived it by finding effective molecules in chemical space that human intuition and conventional screens had missed.
