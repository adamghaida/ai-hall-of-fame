# 📚 Context: Coscientist — a GPT-4 agent that designs and runs its own experiments

Coscientist is a landmark for *agentic* AI in science: the model isn't just answering questions, it's taking real-world actions. It also sharpened the safety conversation — the authors deliberately probed whether such systems could be misused to synthesize dangerous compounds, and discussed guardrails.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It closed the loop from natural-language goal → literature reasoning → experimental design → physical execution, a milestone toward self-driving laboratories.
