# 📚 Context: Dermatologist-level skin-cancer classification

An influential early medical-AI result, but also a cautionary tale studied ever since: such models can latch onto spurious cues (rulers, skin-tone imbalance, image artifacts) and underperform on darker skin if training data is skewed. It helped motivate the field's focus on dataset diversity, external validation, and prospective testing.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Skin cancer is the most common human cancer, and a smartphone-grade classifier hinted at scalable early detection far beyond the reach of specialist clinics.
