# Dermatologist-level skin-cancer classification

**Field:** 🩺 Medicine  ·  **When:** 2017 · Nature

> A neural net matched 21 board-certified dermatologists at spotting skin cancer from photos.

## What happened
Stanford researchers (Esteva et al.) trained a deep CNN on **129,450 clinical images** to classify skin lesions. Tested against **21 board-certified dermatologists**, it matched their accuracy at distinguishing malignant from benign lesions — including melanoma and carcinoma.

## Why it matters
Skin cancer is the most common human cancer, and a smartphone-grade classifier hinted at scalable early detection far beyond the reach of specialist clinics.
