# Matching — and beating — radiologists at breast-cancer screening

**Field:** 🩺 Medicine  ·  **When:** January 2020 · Nature

> An AI second reader that caught cancers humans missed and cut false alarms.

## What happened
A Google Health / DeepMind model trained on mammograms from ~30,000 women (US and UK) reduced false positives by **5.7%** (US data), caught **9.4%** of cancers that had been missed, and outperformed six independent radiologists reading unaided.

## Why it matters
Earlier, more accurate detection directly saves lives, and an instant automated 'second opinion' could extend expert-level screening to places without enough radiologists.
