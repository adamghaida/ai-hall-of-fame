# 📚 Context: Matching — and beating — radiologists at breast-cancer screening

Honest caveats the authors themselves flagged: the study was largely retrospective, used mostly one manufacturer's images, and does not replace prospective clinical trials. Its lasting contribution was showing AI could *generalize across countries* and act as an independent reader — later work (e.g. Sweden's MASAI trial) has tested AI-assisted screening prospectively.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Earlier, more accurate detection directly saves lives, and an instant automated 'second opinion' could extend expert-level screening to places without enough radiologists.
