# 📚 Context: An AI reading of a cheap ECG spots a weak heart before symptoms

The network learns subtle, sub-visual ECG patterns that correlate with reduced pumping function. What makes this stand out among medical-AI papers is that it was later validated **prospectively** (the EAGLE trial embedded it in routine primary-care practice), rather than only retrospectively. Caveats: performance varies across populations and it is a screening triage tool, not a diagnosis, so positives still need confirmatory imaging.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It pulls a signal cardiologists cannot see from a ubiquitous, cheap test, opening the door to population-scale screening for a treatable but dangerous condition. It later held up in a prospective, real-world trial.
