# 📚 Context: Detecting diabetic retinopathy at specialist level

This 2016 paper was a proof point that helped ignite the medical-AI wave. It later became one of the first AI diagnostics deployed in the field (screening programs in India and Thailand), which surfaced real-world lessons about image quality, workflow, and the gap between retrospective accuracy and clinic performance.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Diabetic retinopathy is a leading cause of preventable blindness, and screening is bottlenecked by specialist availability. This was an early, rigorous demonstration that AI could deliver expert-level medical image diagnosis at scale.
