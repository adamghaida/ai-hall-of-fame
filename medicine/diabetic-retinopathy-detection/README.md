# Detecting diabetic retinopathy at specialist level

**Field:** 🩺 Medicine  ·  **When:** 2016 · JAMA

> One of the first deep-learning systems to match specialists on a real diagnostic task.

## What happened
Google researchers trained a deep CNN on **128,175 retinal fundus images** to detect **diabetic retinopathy** and macular edema. On validation sets it matched the performance of ophthalmologists, with high sensitivity and specificity.

## Why it matters
Diabetic retinopathy is a leading cause of preventable blindness, and screening is bottlenecked by specialist availability. This was an early, rigorous demonstration that AI could deliver expert-level medical image diagnosis at scale.
