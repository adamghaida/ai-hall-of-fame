# 🩺 Medicine

From reading mammograms to giving a voice back to people who lost the ability to speak.

---

### Matching (and beating) radiologists at breast-cancer screening

- **What happened:** A Google Health / DeepMind model trained on mammograms from ~30,000 women in the US and UK spotted breast cancers that were later confirmed by biopsy. In the study it **reduced false positives by 5.7%** (US data) and **caught 9.4% of cancers that had been missed**, and outperformed six independent radiologists reading unaided.
- **When:** January 2020 (*Nature*).
- **Why it's a big deal:** A credible demonstration that AI could act as an accurate, instant "second reader" in cancer screening — where earlier detection directly saves lives. (Real-world clinical deployment still requires prospective trials.)
- **Sources:** [Nature paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-019-1799-6) · [MIT Tech Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2020/01/03/238154/googles-ai-breast-cancer-screening-tool-is-learning-to-generalize-across-countries/)

### A speech neuroprosthesis that restores conversation after paralysis

- **What happened:** Two 2023 *Nature* studies (Stanford and UCSF/UC Berkeley) used AI to decode neural activity from people with severe paralysis into text and speech. The Stanford system let a participant with ALS communicate at **62 words per minute** — 3.4× the previous record and approaching natural conversational speed — while the UCSF system drove a talking digital **avatar** with facial expressions.
- **When:** August 2023 (*Nature*).
- **Why it's a big deal:** For people locked in by ALS or stroke, AI decoding turned imagined speech back into communication at near-conversational speeds — a life-changing restoration of one of the most human abilities.
- **Sources:** [Stanford / Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06377-x) · [UCSF / Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06443-4) · [NIH summary](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/brain-computer-interface-restores-natural-speech-after-paralysis)

### Halicin — an AI-discovered antibiotic

- **What happened:** A deep-learning screen of 100M+ molecules surfaced halicin, a structurally novel antibiotic effective against drug-resistant bacteria. (Full write-up in [Chemistry](../chemistry/).)
- **When:** February 2020 (*Cell*).
- **Sources:** [Broad Institute](https://www.broadinstitute.org/news/researchers-use-ai-identify-new-class-antibiotic-candidates)
