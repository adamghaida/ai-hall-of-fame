# Rentosertib — a generative-AI-designed drug reaches human trials

**Field:** 🩺 Medicine  ·  **When:** June 2025 · Nature Medicine

> The first drug with both an AI-discovered target and AI-designed molecule to reach late-stage (Phase 3) clinical development.

## What happened
Insilico Medicine used its generative-AI platform to both identify a novel target (**TNIK**) and design the molecule **rentosertib** (ISM001-055) for **idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis**. A randomized **Phase 2a** trial in 71 patients met its safety endpoint and showed a dose-dependent lung-function signal.

On **July 7, 2026**, Insilico announced the **initiation** of a **Phase 3** trial: 320 IPF patients across 47 centers in China, with the annual rate of decline in forced vital capacity over 52 weeks as the primary endpoint (registrations CTR20262475 and NCT07687459; as of early August 2026 ClinicalTrials.gov still lists NCT07687459 as "Not yet recruiting", with an estimated start date of August 30, 2026, so no patient has been enrolled yet). Insilico describes it as "the company's first asset to enter a Phase III clinical trial"; *Chemistry World*, writing a year earlier, reported that no AI-designed drug had ever entered a Phase 3 and that rentosertib looked set to become the first. On that reckoning it is the first drug with both an AI-discovered target and an AI-designed molecule to reach late-stage clinical development. Starting a Phase 3 is not the same as succeeding in one.

## Why it matters
It is a landmark clinical proof-of-concept that AI-driven drug discovery can produce a candidate that behaves well in patients — not just in silico.
