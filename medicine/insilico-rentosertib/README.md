# Rentosertib — a generative-AI-designed drug reaches human trials

**Field:** 🩺 Medicine  ·  **When:** June 2025 · Nature Medicine

> The first drug with both an AI-discovered target and AI-designed molecule to post Phase 2 results.

## What happened
Insilico Medicine used its generative-AI platform to both identify a novel target (**TNIK**) and design the molecule **rentosertib** (ISM001-055) for **idiopathic pulmonary fibrosis**. A randomized **Phase 2a** trial in 71 patients met its safety endpoint and showed a dose-dependent lung-function signal; a Phase 3 trial began in 2026.

## Why it matters
It is a landmark clinical proof-of-concept that AI-driven drug discovery can produce a candidate that behaves well in patients — not just in silico.
