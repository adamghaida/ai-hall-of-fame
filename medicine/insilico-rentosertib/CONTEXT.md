# 📚 Context: Rentosertib — a generative-AI-designed drug reaches human trials

Read the claim carefully: 'AI-designed' means AI proposed the target and lead molecule; humans still ran medicinal chemistry, tox studies, and the clinical trial. The Phase 2a signal is promising but early (small, China-based, 12 weeks). Still, it's the clearest evidence yet that the generative-chemistry pipeline can reach and clear clinical hurdles.

The July 2026 Phase 3 announcement is the biggest caveat to read carefully. Starting a Phase 3 is not succeeding in one — Phase 3 is where a great many plausible-looking candidates fail, and nothing about the trial's initiation says anything about whether rentosertib works. Nor has this trial actually begun: as of early August 2026 ClinicalTrials.gov lists NCT07687459 as "Not yet recruiting", with an estimated start date of August 30, 2026. The primary endpoint is measured over 52 weeks, so the first efficacy readout is years away. What the milestone does establish is that an AI-discovered target and an AI-designed molecule can get this far.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It is a landmark clinical proof-of-concept that AI-driven drug discovery can produce a candidate that behaves well in patients — not just in silico.
