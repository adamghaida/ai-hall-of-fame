# 📚 Context: Rentosertib — a generative-AI-designed drug reaches human trials

Read the claim carefully: 'AI-designed' means AI proposed the target and lead molecule; humans still ran medicinal chemistry, tox studies, and the clinical trial. The Phase 2a signal is promising but early (small, China-based, 12 weeks). Still, it's the clearest evidence yet that the generative-chemistry pipeline can reach and clear clinical hurdles.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It is a landmark clinical proof-of-concept that AI-driven drug discovery can produce a candidate that behaves well in patients — not just in silico.
