# A speech neuroprosthesis that restores conversation after paralysis

**Field:** 🩺 Medicine  ·  **When:** August 2023 · Nature

> AI decoded imagined speech back into words at near-conversational speed.

## What happened
Two 2023 *Nature* studies (Stanford; UCSF/UC Berkeley) used deep-learning decoders to translate neural activity from people with severe paralysis into text and speech. The Stanford system reached **62 words per minute** — 3.4× the previous record — for a participant with ALS; the UCSF system decoded speech and drove an expressive talking **avatar** for a participant paralyzed by a brainstem stroke.

## Why it matters
For people locked in by ALS or stroke, AI decoding restored communication at speeds approaching natural conversation — a life-changing return of one of the most human abilities.
