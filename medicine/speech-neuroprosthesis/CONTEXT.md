# 📚 Context: A speech neuroprosthesis that restores conversation after paralysis

These systems decode *attempted* speech (motor cortex activity for trying to speak), not abstract 'thoughts'. Performance depends on stable high-density recordings and per-person calibration. A 2025 follow-up produced streaming brain-to-voice synthesis in the participant's own restored voice. Cross-listed under [Neuroscience](../../neuroscience/).

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
For people locked in by ALS or stroke, AI decoding restored communication at speeds approaching natural conversation — a life-changing return of one of the most human abilities.
