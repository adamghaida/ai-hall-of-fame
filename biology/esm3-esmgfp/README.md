# ESM3 — a glowing protein equivalent to 500 million years of evolution

**Field:** 🧬 Biology  ·  **When:** June 2024 · Science (2025)

> A language model 'invented' a new fluorescent protein far from anything in nature.

## What happened
EvolutionaryScale's **ESM3**, a generative protein language model, designed **esmGFP**, a new green fluorescent protein that differs by 96 of 229 amino acids from its closest natural relative. The team estimated that reaching such a distant, still-functional variant would take **>500 million years** of natural evolution.

## Why it matters
It demonstrated that large models trained on protein sequence, structure, and function can be *prompted* to generate working proteins deep in unexplored regions of protein space.
