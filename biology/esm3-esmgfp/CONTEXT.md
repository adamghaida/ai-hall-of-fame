# 📚 Context: ESM3 — a glowing protein equivalent to 500 million years of evolution

ESM3 sits in the 'protein language model' lineage (ESM-1b, ESM-2, ESMFold). The '500 million years' figure is an evolutionary-distance estimate, not a literal claim, but it vividly captures how far outside the natural distribution a functional design can be reached — the generative analogue of AlphaFold's predictive power.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It demonstrated that large models trained on protein sequence, structure, and function can be *prompted* to generate working proteins deep in unexplored regions of protein space.
