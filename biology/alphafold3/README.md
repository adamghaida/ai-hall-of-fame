# AlphaFold3 — the structure of life's molecules, together

**Field:** 🧬 Biology  ·  **When:** May 2024 · Nature

> Beyond single proteins: predicting how proteins, DNA, RNA, and drug-like molecules actually fit together.

## What happened
AlphaFold3, from Google DeepMind and Isomorphic Labs, replaced AlphaFold2's structure module with a **diffusion-based** architecture and extended prediction to full **complexes**: proteins with nucleic acids, ligands, ions, and modified residues. It substantially beat specialized docking and nucleic-acid tools on their own turf.

## Why it matters
Biology happens through interactions, not single static proteins. Accurately predicting protein-ligand and protein-DNA/RNA complexes is central to drug discovery, which is Isomorphic Labs' entire premise.
