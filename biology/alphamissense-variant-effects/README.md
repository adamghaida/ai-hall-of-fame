# AlphaMissense — classifying 71 million genetic mutations

**Field:** 🧬 Biology  ·  **When:** September 2023 · Science

> A single model rated the disease risk of nearly every possible human missense mutation.

## What happened
DeepMind adapted AlphaFold into **AlphaMissense**, which predicts the pathogenicity of all **71 million possible missense variants** in the human proteome, classifying 89% as likely benign or likely pathogenic — a huge leap given only ~0.1% of these had been clinically annotated.

## Why it matters
Interpreting variants of unknown significance is a central bottleneck in diagnosing rare genetic disease; a proteome-wide, calibrated catalogue is a major resource for clinicians and researchers.
