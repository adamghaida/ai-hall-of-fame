# 🧬 Biology

AI didn't just read the language of life — it started writing in it.

| Case | When | In one line |
|------|------|-------------|
| **[RFdiffusion — designing brand-new proteins from scratch](./rfdiffusion-protein-design/)** | 2022–2023 · Nature | Generative AI that invents functional proteins with folds never seen in nature. |
| **[AlphaMissense — classifying 71 million genetic mutations](./alphamissense-variant-effects/)** | September 2023 · Science | A single model rated the disease risk of nearly every possible human missense mutation. |
| **[ESM3 — a glowing protein equivalent to 500 million years of evolution](./esm3-esmgfp/)** | June 2024 · Science (2025) | A language model 'invented' a new fluorescent protein far from anything in nature. |
| **[AlphaFold3 — the structure of life's molecules, together](./alphafold3/)** | May 2024 · Nature | Beyond single proteins: predicting how proteins, DNA, RNA, and drug-like molecules actually fit together. |
| **[AI co-scientist reaches a decade-long phage discovery in days](./ai-co-scientist-cf-pici/)** | September 2025 · Cell | Asked an unpublished question, Google's multi-agent system top-ranked the mechanism a lab had spent years proving. Retrospective, and a hypothesis rather than an experiment. |
| **[The Virtual Lab — a team of AI agents that designed real SARS-CoV-2 nanobodies](./virtual-lab-nanobodies/)** | July 2025 · Nature | An LLM PI and its specialist agents chose the tools, wrote the pipeline and produced 92 candidates; two beat their parent nanobodies on JN.1 or KP.3. |
| **[Designed antitoxins — AI proteins that neutralize snake venom toxins in mice](./snake-antitoxin-proteins/)** | January 2025 · Nature | Miniproteins designed against three-finger toxins bind at 0.9–1.9 nM, survive above 95 °C, and rescued mice from lethal neurotoxin doses. |
| **[De novo serine hydrolases — enzymes with real active sites, built from a description](./designed-serine-hydrolases/)** | February 2025 · Science | RFdiffusion plus an ensemble network that scores the active site at every step of the mechanism; kcat/Km up to 2.2 × 10⁵ M⁻¹s⁻¹ in five non-natural folds. |
| **[Chai-2 — de novo antibodies at a 16% hit rate, twenty designs per target](./chai-2-antibody-design/)** | July 2025 · preprint | A binder for half of 52 antibody-free targets from ≤20 designs each, in one round. Preprint, company-run benchmark. |
| **[OpenCRISPR-1 — the first AI-generated gene editor to edit the human genome](./opencrispr-1/)** | July 2025 · Nature | A language model wrote a Cas9-like nuclease 403 mutations from SpCas9; it out-edits the original with ~95% fewer off-target cuts, and the sequence is public. |
| **[AlphaProteo — protein binders designed on demand, in one round](./alphaproteo-binders/)** | September 2024 · DeepMind | Binders for seven targets with 3–300× better affinity than prior methods, from a single screening round, plus a published failure on the eighth. |
| **[AlphaGenome — reading a million bases of DNA at base-pair resolution](./alphagenome/)** | January 2026 · Nature | One model predicts thousands of functional genomic tracks from 1 Mb of DNA, extending variant scoring into the non-coding genome. |
| **[SynTnpBs — AI-designed compact genome editors that beat the natural enzyme](./ai-designed-genome-editors/)** | July 2026 · Science | An inverse-folding model rewrote a miniature CRISPR-like nuclease; some synthetic versions out-edited the wild type in human cells. |
| **[AI-redesigned starting points make laboratory evolution work better](./ai-primed-directed-evolution/)** | July 2026 · Nature | Stabilize the enzyme with a neural network first, and directed evolution goes 79-fold further than it does from the natural protein. |
| **[Evo — genome language models that wrote working bacteriophages](./evo-designed-bacteriophages/)** | August 2026 · Science | 285 AI-written viral genomes were synthesized; 16 produced infectious phages, and a cocktail of them beat ΦX174-resistant *E. coli*. |
