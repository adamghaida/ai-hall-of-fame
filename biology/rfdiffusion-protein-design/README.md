# RFdiffusion — designing brand-new proteins from scratch

**Field:** 🧬 Biology  ·  **When:** 2022–2023 · Nature

> Generative AI that invents functional proteins with folds never seen in nature.

## What happened
David Baker's lab built **RFdiffusion**, a generative diffusion model that designs proteins *de novo* — including topologies not found in nature — up to ~600 residues. Hundreds of AI-designed proteins were experimentally validated as potential medicines, vaccines, and nanomaterials. Baker shared the **2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry** for computational protein design.

## Why it matters
Diffusion models pushed lab success rates from roughly 1-in-10,000 to a few percent, turning protein design from a rare art into a repeatable engineering discipline.
