# 📚 Context: RFdiffusion — designing brand-new proteins from scratch

RFdiffusion adapted the same denoising-diffusion idea behind image generators to protein backbones, conditioned on the structure-prediction network RoseTTAFold. The design-then-validate stack (RFdiffusion → ProteinMPNN → AF2) has become a standard modern pipeline, and the lab has since released RFdiffusion3 for designing binders to DNA, small molecules, and more.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Diffusion models pushed lab success rates from roughly 1-in-10,000 to a few percent, turning protein design from a rare art into a repeatable engineering discipline.
