# AlphaCode — competitive programming at the median human level

**Field:** 💻 Computer Science  ·  **When:** December 2022 · Science

> The first AI to hold its own in real, live competitive-programming contests.

## What happened
**AlphaCode** generated solutions to unseen competitive-programming problems by sampling millions of candidate programs and filtering them against the problems' examples. Across 10 recent **Codeforces** contests with 5,000+ participants each, it placed in roughly the **top 54%** — the level of the median competitor.

## Why it matters
Competitive programming demands reading a natural-language puzzle and inventing a correct, efficient algorithm — a reasoning task, not a lookup. Reaching median-human performance was a landmark for code generation.
