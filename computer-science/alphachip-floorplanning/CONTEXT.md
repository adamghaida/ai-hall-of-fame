# 📚 Context: AlphaChip — AI that lays out the chips it runs on

Honest note: AlphaChip drew public **controversy** — a 2023 critique questioned the benchmarks, and Nature added an editorial note before ultimately standing by the paper. DeepMind published a rebuttal ('That Chip Has Sailed') and the strongest evidence is deployment: the method placed blocks in shipping TPU designs. A good case study in how contested even real breakthroughs can be.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Physical design is a critical, labor-intensive bottleneck in making faster chips — the hardware that in turn trains bigger AI. AlphaChip closed part of that loop.
