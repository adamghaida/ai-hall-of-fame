# Symbolic transformers find Lyapunov functions

**Field:** 🧮 Mathematics  ·  **When:** 2024 · NeurIPS

> AI made progress on a problem with no known general solution — proving systems are stable.

## What happened
Meta AI researchers trained sequence-to-sequence **symbolic transformers** to discover **Lyapunov functions**, which certify the global stability of a dynamical system. The models outperformed both algorithmic solvers and humans on polynomial systems and discovered new Lyapunov functions for non-polynomial systems where no general method exists.

## Why it matters
Finding a Lyapunov function is a long-standing open problem with no general algorithm; a learned model that proposes them (each easily checked) is a real tool for control theory and mathematics.
