# 📚 Context: Guiding human intuition — a new knot-theory theorem

A crucial honest point: the AI did not prove anything — it acted as a hypothesis generator over spaces too large for humans to eyeball. The mathematicians (Juhász, Lackenby, Williamson) did the proving. This 'centaur' model is arguably more broadly applicable than fully-automated theorem proving.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
This established a repeatable template for AI-assisted pure mathematics: the machine surfaces non-obvious patterns; the human supplies rigorous proof.
