# Guiding human intuition — a new knot-theory theorem

**Field:** 🧮 Mathematics  ·  **When:** December 2021 · Nature

> AI spotted patterns that led mathematicians to prove brand-new theorems.

## What happened
DeepMind worked with mathematicians at Oxford and Sydney to use machine learning as an 'intuition' tool: models found candidate relationships in large mathematical datasets, which humans then turned into proofs. The result was a **new theorem connecting algebraic and geometric invariants of knots**, plus progress on the combinatorial invariance conjecture in representation theory.

## Why it matters
This established a repeatable template for AI-assisted pure mathematics: the machine surfaces non-obvious patterns; the human supplies rigorous proof.
