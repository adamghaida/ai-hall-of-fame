# 📚 Context: AlphaEvolve — breaking Strassen again, and improving ~50 open problems

AlphaEvolve is best understood as the maturation of the FunSearch idea into an industrial tool: stronger base model, richer evaluators, and deployment on real engineering problems inside Google. Its math wins are notable precisely because they were a *side effect* of a general optimizer, not a bespoke math system.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Where AlphaTensor was specialized, AlphaEvolve is general: it evolves arbitrary code evaluated by any scorer, letting one system improve mathematics, hardware scheduling, and chip-design subroutines alike.
