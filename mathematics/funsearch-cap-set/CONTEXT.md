# 📚 Context: FunSearch — a genuinely new answer to a long-open math problem

The framing matters: FunSearch searches over *programs that construct solutions*, not the solutions themselves, so a human can read and verify the discovered heuristic. It works best where solutions are easy to check but hard to find — the classic profile of many combinatorics and algorithm-design problems.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Rather than retrieving a known result, FunSearch generated mathematical knowledge that did not previously exist — and because candidates are programs scored by an evaluator, the output is verifiable.
