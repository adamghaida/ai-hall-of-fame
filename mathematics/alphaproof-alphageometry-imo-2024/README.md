# AlphaProof & AlphaGeometry 2 — silver-medal math at the IMO

**Field:** 🧮 Mathematics  ·  **When:** July 2024 · Nature (2025)

> The first time a machine reached medal-level performance at the world's premier mathematics competition.

## What happened
DeepMind's AlphaProof (which writes proofs in the formal language **Lean** and trains by reinforcement learning) together with AlphaGeometry 2 solved 4 of the 6 problems at the 2024 International Mathematical Olympiad, scoring 28/42 — equal to a **silver medalist** and one point shy of gold. AlphaProof cracked the notoriously hard Problem 6, which only 5 of ~600 human contestants solved.

## Why it matters
Because every proof is checked by the Lean proof assistant, the answers are **provably correct**, not plausible guesses. It was the first AI to reach medal-level performance at the IMO.
