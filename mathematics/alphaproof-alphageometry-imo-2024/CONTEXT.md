# 📚 Context: AlphaProof & AlphaGeometry 2 — silver-medal math at the IMO

Formal proof (Lean, Coq, Isabelle) had long been seen as too slow for competition-style creativity. AlphaProof's contribution was pairing an LLM auto-formalizer with RL search over the Lean proof space, sidestepping the fact that there is very little human-written formal training data. Its weakness mirrored humans': the two combinatorics problems, which are hard to formalize and blow up the search, went unsolved. Collaborators framed it internally as their 'Apollo program' for reasoning.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Because every proof is checked by the Lean proof assistant, the answers are **provably correct**, not plausible guesses. It was the first AI to reach medal-level performance at the IMO.
