# Gemini Deep Think — an official IMO gold medal

**Field:** 🧮 Mathematics  ·  **When:** July 2025

> One year after silver, an AI hit gold — working end-to-end in natural language.

## What happened
An advanced version of **Gemini with 'Deep Think'** solved 5 of 6 problems for 35/42 points at the 2025 IMO, earning an **official gold medal** under the same rules and time limits as human contestants — and doing it directly in natural language rather than a formal proof language.

## Why it matters
Gold at the IMO in plain natural language (no Lean formalization step) signaled that general-purpose reasoning models, not just specialized provers, had reached elite mathematical problem-solving.
