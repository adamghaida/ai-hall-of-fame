# 📚 Context: Gemini Deep Think — an official IMO gold medal

Both Google DeepMind and OpenAI reported gold-medal-level results on IMO 2025 within days of each other. A key methodological debate: Google's result was graded and certified under the IMO's official cooperation, while some claims elsewhere were self-graded — a reminder that *how* an AI result is evaluated matters as much as the score.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Gold at the IMO in plain natural language (no Lean formalization step) signaled that general-purpose reasoning models, not just specialized provers, had reached elite mathematical problem-solving.
