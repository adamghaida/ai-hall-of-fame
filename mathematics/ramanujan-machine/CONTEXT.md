# 📚 Context: The Ramanujan Machine — a conjecture generator for fundamental constants

This is symbolic/numeric search rather than deep learning, but it is a landmark in *automated conjecture generation*. Its output is a stream of testable hypotheses; several have since been proved by the community, validating the machine as a genuine engine of new mathematics.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It flips the usual direction of discovery: instead of proving known formulas, the machine proposes new mathematical truths for humans to prove, echoing the intuition of Srinivasa Ramanujan.
