# AlphaGeometry — olympiad geometry at gold-medal level

**Field:** 🧮 Mathematics  ·  **When:** January 2024 · Nature

> A neuro-symbolic AI solved hard olympiad geometry nearly as well as a human gold medalist, with no human-written solutions to learn from.

## What happened
AlphaGeometry pairs a language model (trained from scratch on 100 million synthetic theorems) with a symbolic deduction engine. On a benchmark of 30 olympiad geometry problems it solved **25**, close to the average human gold medalist's 25.9 and far above the previous best automated system's 10. The language model proposes useful auxiliary constructions; the symbolic engine does the rigorous deduction.

## Why it matters
Geometry proofs hinge on creative auxiliary constructions that pure search struggles to find. AlphaGeometry showed a neuro-symbolic combination could reach elite competition level with no human demonstrations, and it became the geometry half of the 2024 IMO silver-medal result.
