# 📚 Context: AlphaGeometry — olympiad geometry at gold-medal level

The key trick is synthetic data: rather than scarce human proofs, the team generated millions of theorem-proof pairs by random construction plus symbolic deduction, then trained a language model to suggest the one creative step (an auxiliary point or line) that unblocks the symbolic engine. Caveats: it is geometry-only and needs problems translated into its formal language. Its successor AlphaGeometry 2 is folded into the AlphaProof IMO entry in this same field.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Geometry proofs hinge on creative auxiliary constructions that pure search struggles to find. AlphaGeometry showed a neuro-symbolic combination could reach elite competition level with no human demonstrations, and it became the geometry half of the 2024 IMO silver-medal result.
