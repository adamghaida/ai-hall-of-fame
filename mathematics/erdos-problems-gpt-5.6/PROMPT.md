# 🤖 Explore this with AI: six open Erdős problems solved in five days with GPT-5.6

Paste the prompt below into your favorite AI assistant (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, or a local model). If your assistant can browse the web, tell it to read the sources first. The point is a clear, honest, grounded explanation of what happened and why it mattered.

> One researcher, thirteen attempts, six previously-open problems from Paul Erdős's list closed in under a week using a structured prompting workflow with GPT-5.6.

---

```text
You are a sharp, honest math and AI-workflow explainer. Walk me through a specific, real AI-assisted result so that I actually understand it, not just the headline.

Topic: Shouqiao Wang's claim of solving 6 previously-open Erdős problems (390, 486, 536, 788, 1002, 1038) in 5 days using GPT-5.6 Sol via a Codex-based workflow, announced July 22, 2026.

Please cover, in plain language a curious non-expert can follow:
1. What erdosproblems.com is and what it means for a problem to be listed as "open" there.
2. What the reported workflow actually did differently from just asking a model "solve this": precise problem restatement, explicit success criteria, ruling out near-miss weaker results, pre-loading known traps, and adversarial multi-agent checking.
3. How this fits the broader recent pattern of AI contributing to Erdos problems (e.g. Problem 1196 verified by Terence Tao, Mark Sellke's work with GPT-5).
4. The honest caveats: this is a very fresh, largely self-reported claim; peer/community verification of this specific batch is still pending; Lean formalization is in progress, not complete; and the six problems likely vary a lot in depth and difficulty.
5. What a skeptical mathematician would want to check before accepting the claim, and where to look (the GitHub repo with the actual proofs).

Ground your answer in these sources (read them if you can, and cite them as you go):
- Announcement thread (X): https://x.com/Qiaoqiao2001/status/2080003441821163958
- Proofs, prompts, and LaTeX source (GitHub): https://github.com/ShouqiaoW/erdos
- Erdos Problems database: https://www.erdosproblems.com/
- AI contributions to Erdos problems (tracking wiki): https://github.com/teorth/erdosproblems/wiki/AI-contributions-to-Erd%C5%91s-problems

Be concrete, clearly separate confirmed facts from unverified claims, and if you are unsure about a detail, say so instead of guessing.
```
