# Teaching a bipedal robot to play soccer

**Field:** 🤖 Robotics  ·  **When:** April 2024 · Science Robotics

> Deep RL gave toy-sized humanoids agile, emergent soccer skills: walking, kicking, blocking, and getting back up.

## What happened
DeepMind trained **20-joint bipedal robots** to play 1v1 soccer end-to-end with deep reinforcement learning in simulation, then transferred the policies **zero-shot** to real hardware. The agents showed emergent skills (rapid fall-recovery, turning, kicking) and rudimentary tactics like anticipating the ball and blocking shots, moving far more dynamically than scripted controllers.

## Why it matters
Whole-body agile control of a bipedal robot is hard, and transferring dynamic, contact-rich skills from simulation to the real world is a long-standing challenge. This showed learned full-body control with genuine robustness.
