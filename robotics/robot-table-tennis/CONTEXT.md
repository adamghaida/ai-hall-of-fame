# 📚 Context: A robot that plays amateur-level competitive table tennis

The headline lesson is **sim-to-real** plus **hierarchy**: individual strokes learned in simulation, composed by a policy that reads the opponent and ball state. Its clear failure mode — losing to advanced players due to reaction latency — honestly maps the current frontier of fast, reactive robotic control.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Table tennis compresses perception, prediction, and high-speed precise control into fractions of a second against an adaptive opponent — a demanding testbed for real-world, real-time robot learning.
