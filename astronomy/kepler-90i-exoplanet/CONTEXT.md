# 📚 Context: Kepler-90i — a neural network finds a hidden eighth planet

This was ML as a *rescoring* tool: the transit signals were in existing data; the network provided the sensitivity and speed to confidently surface faint ones. The approach has since scaled to TESS and other surveys, where the data volume makes automated vetting essential.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
The AI caught a signal too faint for earlier searches and did in minutes what took a human researcher hours — a template for mining vast survey datasets for discoveries hiding in the noise.
