# Kepler-90i — a neural network finds a hidden eighth planet

**Field:** 🔭 Astronomy  ·  **When:** December 2017

> AI recovered a planet too faint for prior searches, tying our Solar System's planet count.

## What happened
Christopher Shallue (Google) and astronomer Andrew Vanderburg trained a neural network on ~15,000 vetted Kepler signals, then aimed it at 670 known multi-planet systems to hunt for weak transit dips humans had missed. It flagged **Kepler-90i**, making **Kepler-90 the first star besides the Sun known to host eight planets**.

## Why it matters
The AI caught a signal too faint for earlier searches and did in minutes what took a human researcher hours — a template for mining vast survey datasets for discoveries hiding in the noise.
