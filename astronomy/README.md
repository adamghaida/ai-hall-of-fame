### Kepler-90i — a neural network finds an eighth planet

- **What happened:** Google researcher Christopher Shallue and astronomer Andrew Vanderburg trained a neural network on ~15,000 vetted signals from NASA's Kepler telescope, then aimed it at 670 already-studied star systems to hunt for weak transit dips humans had missed. It flagged **Kepler-90i**, a hot rocky world 2,545 light-years away — making **Kepler-90 the first star besides our Sun known to host eight planets**.
- **When:** December 2017.
- **Why it's a big deal:** The AI recovered a signal too faint for prior searches to confidently catch, and did in minutes what took a human researcher eight hours — a template for mining enormous astronomical datasets for discoveries hiding in the noise.
- **Sources:** [NASA press release](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/artificial-intelligence-nasa-data-used-to-discover-eighth-planet-circling-distant-star/) · [UT Austin](https://news.utexas.edu/2017/12/14/using-ai-to-discover-new-worlds/)
