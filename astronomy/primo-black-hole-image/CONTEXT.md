# 📚 Context: PRIMO — a sharper image of the M87 black hole

A subtlety worth stating plainly: PRIMO's sharper image is a *reconstruction* that leans on simulated priors, so it reflects both the data and assumptions about accretion physics — the team validated that it stays consistent with the measurements. It's a good example of learned priors making the most of expensive, incomplete observations.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Radio-interferometer data is inherently sparse; PRIMO used learned priors about what accretion actually looks like to recover structure the raw data undersampled, improving one of the most iconic images in science.
