# PRIMO — a sharper image of the M87 black hole

**Field:** 🔭 Astronomy  ·  **When:** April 2023 · Astrophysical Journal

> Machine learning filled in the gaps to reach the full resolution of the black-hole telescope.

## What happened
The team behind the first black-hole image applied **PRIMO**, a machine-learning technique trained on **30,000+ simulated images** of gas accreting onto a black hole, to the Event Horizon Telescope's 2017 data of **M87**. It reconstructed a sharper 'skinny donut' image at the array's full resolution.

## Why it matters
Radio-interferometer data is inherently sparse; PRIMO used learned priors about what accretion actually looks like to recover structure the raw data undersampled, improving one of the most iconic images in science.
