# 📚 Context: GNoME — 2.2 million new crystals, 380,000 of them stable

GNoME's predictions are computational (DFT-level stability), so 'discovered' means 'predicted stable and added to a database', with hundreds since verified experimentally. It sparked healthy debate about how many predicted crystals are truly novel and synthesizable — a reminder that in silico discovery is the start of the pipeline, not the end.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It expanded known stable materials by ~an order of magnitude — described as ~800 years of prior discovery in one effort — giving experimentalists a vast new menu to synthesize.
