# GNoME — 2.2 million new crystals, 380,000 of them stable

**Field:** 🔬 Materials Science  ·  **When:** November 2023 · Nature

> An order-of-magnitude expansion of the stable materials known to science.

## What happened
DeepMind's **Graph Networks for Materials Exploration (GNoME)** predicted the stability of new inorganic crystals at scale, discovering **2.2 million** structures — including **380,000 stable candidates** for batteries, solar cells, and more. Roughly 380,000 were released into the public **Materials Project**.

## Why it matters
It expanded known stable materials by ~an order of magnitude — described as ~800 years of prior discovery in one effort — giving experimentalists a vast new menu to synthesize.
