# 📚 Context: An AI-screened battery electrolyte using ~70% less lithium

A candid caveat from the team: the new electrolyte is a proof-of-concept, not yet competitive with the best commercial materials, and may not scale. The significance is the *speed and funnel* — reducing tens of millions of candidates to a handful of synthesizable ones fast enough to test — rather than the specific material.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It showed an end-to-end AI+HPC funnel — from massive virtual screening to a physically made, functional battery material — compressing a search that traditionally takes years.
