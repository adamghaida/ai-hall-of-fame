# An AI-screened battery electrolyte using ~70% less lithium

**Field:** 🔬 Materials Science  ·  **When:** January 2024

> From 32 million candidates to a synthesized new material in weeks, not years.

## What happened
Microsoft and the Pacific Northwest National Laboratory used AI plus high-performance computing to narrow **32 million** candidate materials down to **18** promising solid-state electrolytes in ~80 hours, then synthesized a working one that uses up to **70% less lithium** than conventional designs.

## Why it matters
It showed an end-to-end AI+HPC funnel — from massive virtual screening to a physically made, functional battery material — compressing a search that traditionally takes years.
