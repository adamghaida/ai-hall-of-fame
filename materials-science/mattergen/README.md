# MatterGen — generating new materials to order

**Field:** 🔬 Materials Science  ·  **When:** January 2025 · Nature

> Instead of screening millions of candidates, describe the properties you want and let a diffusion model design the crystal.

## What happened
Microsoft's **MatterGen** is a diffusion model that generates novel inorganic crystals, jointly designing composition, atomic positions, and the unit cell, and can be fine-tuned to hit target constraints: chemistry, symmetry, and mechanical, electronic, or magnetic properties. Its outputs are more than twice as likely to be novel-and-stable as prior generative models, and the team synthesized one generated compound to validate the approach.

## Why it matters
It flips materials discovery from **screening** (search a fixed list) to **generation** (design on demand), which pairs naturally with screening engines like GNoME and autonomous synthesis labs.
