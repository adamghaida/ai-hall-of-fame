# 📚 Context: A-Lab — a robotic lab that makes new materials on its own

Important caveat: A-Lab drew scientific scrutiny. Independent crystallographers questioned how many of the 41 were genuinely new phases versus mis-identified known ones, and Nature issued a **correction** in 2024. Included here honestly — as both a real milestone in lab autonomy *and* a case study in why AI-generated results need rigorous human verification.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
It demonstrated a genuinely self-driving experimental loop for materials — pairing naturally with computational discovery engines like GNoME that generate targets faster than humans can make them.
