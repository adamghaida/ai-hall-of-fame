# A-Lab — a robotic lab that makes new materials on its own

**Field:** 🔬 Materials Science  ·  **When:** November 2023 · Nature

> An autonomous lab that synthesized dozens of new inorganic compounds in 17 days.

## What happened
Berkeley Lab's **A-Lab** combines computation, literature-mined data, machine learning, and robotics to plan and run solid-state syntheses with little human input. Over 17 days it produced **41 new inorganic compounds** out of 58 targets — running ~21 experiments a day and using active learning to adjust recipes after failures.

## Why it matters
It demonstrated a genuinely self-driving experimental loop for materials — pairing naturally with computational discovery engines like GNoME that generate targets faster than humans can make them.
