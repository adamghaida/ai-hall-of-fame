# 📚 Context: AI Feynman — rediscovering the laws of physics from data

Symbolic regression differs sharply from black-box ML: the output is a human-readable formula you can reason about and extrapolate from. AI Feynman helped kick off a wave of interest in interpretable 'AI scientist' methods, later joined by sparse-regression (SINDy) and transformer-based approaches.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Symbolic regression is essentially NP-hard, yet recovering compact, interpretable laws from data is exactly what a scientist does. AI Feynman showed the search is tractable when you use the structure real physics tends to have.
