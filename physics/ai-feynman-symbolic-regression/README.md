# AI Feynman — rediscovering the laws of physics from data

**Field:** ⚛️ Physics  ·  **When:** April 2020 · Science Advances

> An algorithm that re-derived 100 equations from the Feynman Lectures straight from raw data.

## What happened
Udrescu and Tegmark's **AI Feynman** tackles symbolic regression — finding the equation behind a dataset. By exploiting physics-flavored structure (symmetry, separability, compositionality, units) it rediscovered all **100 equations** from the Feynman Lectures on Physics and pushed the state of the art on a harder benchmark from a 15% to a 90% success rate.

## Why it matters
Symbolic regression is essentially NP-hard, yet recovering compact, interpretable laws from data is exactly what a scientist does. AI Feynman showed the search is tractable when you use the structure real physics tends to have.
