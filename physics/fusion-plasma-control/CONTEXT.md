# 📚 Context: Controlling fusion plasma inside a real tokamak

The headline is sim-to-real transfer on a system where mistakes are expensive: the controller learned entirely in simulation yet worked on the physical reactor. TCV runs are only ~2 seconds (thermal limits), so the demonstration was necessarily short — but it proved learned control can match bespoke controllers across many configurations at once.

## Why it's in the Hall of Fame
Magnetically confining plasma at ~100-million-degree temperatures is one of the hardest control problems in science and central to clean fusion energy. A single learned controller replaced painstakingly hand-designed systems.
