# Controlling fusion plasma inside a real tokamak

**Field:** ⚛️ Physics  ·  **When:** February 2022 · Nature

> Reinforcement learning drove a real nuclear-fusion reactor's magnetic coils on its first try.

## What happened
DeepMind and EPFL's Swiss Plasma Center trained a **deep reinforcement-learning** controller for the magnetic coils of the **TCV tokamak**, then deployed it on the real machine. The AI held superheated plasma steady and sculpted it into varied shapes — elongated, 'snowflake', negative-triangularity — and even sustained two separate plasma 'droplets' at once, with no fine-tuning on the hardware.

## Why it matters
Magnetically confining plasma at ~100-million-degree temperatures is one of the hardest control problems in science and central to clean fusion energy. A single learned controller replaced painstakingly hand-designed systems.
