04Criteria

An entry has to meet four conditions.

Documented. Backed by a peer-reviewed paper, an official research release, or credible reporting. Every entry links at least one primary source.

Concrete. A specific result: a record broken, an open problem resolved, a first of its kind, or a real deployment. Not a general claim about AI transforming a field.

Notable. Significant within its field.

Honest. Where a result was contested, corrected, or is only a proof of concept, the entry says so.

Unsolved problems are not listed. The Riemann hypothesis and P vs NP have no entry. The Jacobian conjecture was on that list until July 2026, when an AI-assisted counterexample disproved it for n ≥ 3; it now has an entry.

05Contribute

Entries live as markdown folders in the GitHub repository, one folder per case with a record, a context file, and a prompt. This site is generated from those folders. See CONTRIBUTING.md for the format. To report an error, open an issue.
